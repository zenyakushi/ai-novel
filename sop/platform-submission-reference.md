## **2\. Platform Submission Profiles**

### **2.1 GoodNovel**

**Register:** Create an account at goodnovel.com.

**Book setup & minimum content:** Create the book entry and upload at least **5,000 words**, then click "Apply for a contract."

**What editors evaluate:**

* Cover/synopsis review first (10-14 business days): checks for copyright issues and content-policy compliance.  
* Contract decision next (2-4 weeks): editors read your **first 10 chapters** and want to see a **detailed outline of how the story ends**. Submit that outline with your application; don't make them ask for it.

**Format requirements:**

* Recommended chapter length: **0.8k-1k words.**  
* Exclusive contracts currently accepted in: **English, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Filipino.** Stick to one language per submission unless it's an intentional translation entry.

**Content gates:**

* **No fan fiction** of copyrighted, non-public-domain IP. If your premise is inspired by an existing property, confirm it's actually in the public domain (U.S. Copyright Office catalog) before submitting.  
* **Cover restrictions:** no explicit sexual imagery, no violence (guns/knives/blood), no alcohol/tobacco imagery aimed at minors, no unlicensed artwork, no celebrity photos.  
* **Underage authors (under 21):** a parent/legal guardian must co-sign an underage confirmation form; government-issued ID is required (no student IDs, and you can't submit under a guardian's ID without disclosing it).

**Submission checklist:**

* \[ \] Account created, book entry set up  
* \[ \] 5,000+ words uploaded  
* \[ \] First 10 chapters polished: this is what gets read for the contract decision  
* \[ \] Full ending outline written and ready to submit alongside the application  
* \[ \] Cover art cleared against the restrictions above (or no cover yet, if GoodNovel allows submitting without one: confirm with editor 🚩)  
* \[ \] Confirmed the book isn't fan fiction of non-public-domain IP  
* \[ \] Government ID ready if under 21

### **2.2 SevenCat / TapRead**

**Register:** Create an account at sevencat.com (TapRead is the reader-facing app).

**Book setup & minimum content:** 🚩 The source doesn't state a minimum word/chapter count to open an application: it only specifies the 30,000-word threshold that triggers the *signing bonus*, which may or may not be the same as the minimum needed to apply. Confirm directly before assuming 30k is your submission floor.

**What editors evaluate:** Not itemized in the source beyond a general instruction to submit complete materials: SevenCat explicitly warns that incomplete exclusive-contract applications get delayed, so front-load everything you have rather than applying early and filling gaps later.

**Review timeline:**

* Non-exclusive: up to **7 business days.**  
* Exclusive: **at least 14 business days.**

**Two paths in:**

1. **Standard track**: original English novels, Exclusive Contract.  
2. **Top Author Program**: for authors who've already earned **$30,000+ cumulative revenue on any single platform within the past 2 years.** If that's you, register or contact your editor directly for Top Author verification instead of the standard track. You're negotiating a competitive per-1,000-word base fee or an elevated signing fee, not applying cold.

**Content gates:**

* Plagiarism, meaningless/repetitive content, and AI-generated text are grounds for full bonus cancellation, possible work removal, and potential legal consequences: treat this as a submission-integrity gate, not just a post-signing risk.

**Post-signing note that affects your submission plan:** hitting 30,000 words doesn't automatically trigger anything: you must separately **apply to "go premium"** after reaching it. Build that into your plan now so you're not caught off guard later.

**Submission checklist:**

* \[ \] Account created at sevencat.com  
* \[ \] Confirmed whether you qualify for Top Author track ($30k+ earned elsewhere in the past 2 years): if yes, contact your editor for verification instead of the standard application  
* \[ \] Application materials as complete as possible before submitting (given the 14+ business day exclusive review)  
* \[ \] Original English content, no AI-generated or plagiarized text  
* \[ \] Plan noted to apply for "premium" status once you cross 30,000 words: it's not automatic

### **2.4 LetterLux**

**Structural note before you submit anything:** LetterLux isn't a single reading app: it's a distributor. One contract with LetterLux pushes your book live across **9 partner apps simultaneously** (Novellair, Literie, Novelbar, Metronovel, LeReader, NovelOasis, Novelenders, EasyNovel, YumRead). Decide if that model fits your strategy before applying, "exclusive" here means exclusive to the LetterLux network, not to one single app.

**Register:** Create an account with LetterLux.

**Book setup & minimum content:**

1. Write **5,000 words** minimum, submit for review.  
2. If not approved at that stage, LetterLux may invite you to extend to **30,000 words** for a second review pass.  
3. Sign the contract once approved.

**What editors/readers weigh most:** your **first 6 chapters (\~5,000 words)** are the highest-leverage part of the manuscript: they're what's shown free to readers and what LetterLux uses to gauge signability. Treat them as your strongest material, not a slow warm-up.

**Genre acceptance: the only platform in this set with an explicit accept/reject list:**

| Genre | Platform demand | Signing difficulty |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Sports Romance | ★★★★★ | ★★★ |
| Werewolf | ★★★★★ | ★★ |
| Young Adult/Teen | ★★★★ | ★★ |
| Billionaire/CEO Romance | ★★★★ | ★★★ |
| Revenge & Comeback | ★★★ | ★★★ |
| Mafia/Dark Romance | ★★★ | ★★★★ |
| Poetry | **Not accepted** | - |
| Political/Historical | **Not accepted** | - |
| Slice of life (no escalation) | **Not accepted** | - |
| Short Story Compilation | **Not accepted** | - |
| Pure Horror | **Not accepted** | - |

**Format requirements:**

* Recommended: **≥1,000 words per chapter**, at least 1 chapter/day once live.  
* **Scheduled Chapter feature** (relevant once you're posting regularly): can't schedule a release within the next 2 hours; chapters must publish in order; editing a scheduled chapter forces immediate publication instead of holding the schedule; all times display in **UTC+0.**

**Content gates:**

* AI permitted only as a supporting tool: fully AI-generated chapters, unedited AI output, AI-inflated word counts, and AI-driven style inconsistency are all explicit violations.  
* Zero tolerance for plagiarism, repeated/meaningless content, and multi-accounting to game the system. LetterLux has final say on any bonus/benefit determination tied to a violation.

**Submission checklist:**

* \[ \] Confirmed you're comfortable with the 9-app distribution model, not a single-app placement  
* \[ \] Premise matches a "recommended" genre, or you've deliberately chosen to attempt one outside that list knowing signing odds are lower  
* \[ \] 5,000 words ready for first submission  
* \[ \] First 6 chapters specifically polished: these carry outsized weight  
* \[ \] Confirmed the work isn't in a "not accepted" genre (Poetry, Political/Historical, non-escalating Slice of Life, Short Story Compilation, Pure Horror)  
* \[ \] AI use, if any, limited to supporting-tool level, not generation

### **2.5 MoboReader**

**Register:** Create an author account at `author.moboreader.com` (browser, desktop or mobile) or via the mobile app: email, pen name, password.

**Book setup:**

1. Log in to the Author Center.  
2. Create the book entry: language, title, blurb, locked-chapter threshold, genre, tag, editor info.  
3. Upload chapters until you hit **both** 10 chapters **and** 10,000 words: both conditions required, not either/or.  
4. Apply for the contract and wait for editor review.

**Genre/theme/tag classification: required, sequential, and specific to MoboReader:**

1. **Theme first:** pick the core theme (Romance sub-themes: CEO/Mafia, Forbidden Love/Age Gap, Campus Love, Dark Romance, Rekindled Love; Werewolf sub-themes: Classic, Hybrid/Cross-Species; Paranormal sub-themes: Vampire, Dragon, Elf/Wizard, Werecreature/Monster; Niche sub-themes: MM Romance, Sports, Streamer/Influencer, Male-Focused Growth, Suspense/Loop).  
2. **Genre second:** map the theme to a genre bucket (e.g., CEO/Mafia/Forbidden Love/Campus Love → Romance/Billionaires/Mafia/Young Adult; Werewolf/Alpha/Fated Mates → Werewolf; Vampire/Dragon/Paranormal/Werecreature → Fantasy; MM Romance → LGBT+; System/Gaming → Gaming/Adventure; Suspense/Horror/Loop → Horror/Sci-fi; Streamer/Modern → Modern; Supernatural → Horror/Fantasy; anything else → Others).  
3. **Tags third:** select **at least 2 tags.** If the work falls under a Niche Genre, **at least 1 tag must be a niche-specific tag** (MM Romance, Omegaverse, Athlete/Sports Romance, Streamer/Influencer Romance, Weak-to-Strong, System/Level-Up, Time Loop, Apocalypse, Suspense/Horror).

**Format requirements:**

* Recommended chapter length: **1,500-2,500 words**: noticeably longer than GoodNovel's or LetterLux's recommendation, so don't reuse a chapter written to another platform's length target without adjusting.

**Content gates (explicit final checklist, straight from the source):**

* \[ \] Correct theme identified (Step 1\)  
* \[ \] Correct genre identified (Step 2\)  
* \[ \] At least 2 tags selected  
* \[ \] At least 1 niche tag, if the work is a Niche Genre  
* \[ \] Work is original English-language content: **not AI-generated, not plagiarized, not fan fiction**  
* 🚩 \[ \] Word-count feasibility against a target date: the source's own example is "Can I complete 200,000 words by August 31?" This reads as tied to a specific contest/cohort, not a standing rule for every submission. Don't treat 200k-by-a-fixed-date as universal without confirming the current live target.

**Submission checklist:**

* \[ \] Author account created at author.moboreader.com  
* \[ \] Book entry created with language, title, blurb, locked-chapter point, genre, tag, editor info filled in  
* \[ \] 10 chapters **and** 10,000 words uploaded (both, not either)  
* \[ \] Theme → Genre → Tags assigned in that order, with a niche tag included if applicable  
* \[ \] Confirmed not AI-generated, not plagiarized, not fan fiction  
* \[ \] Chapters targeting 1,500-2,500 words each, not reused from a shorter-format platform unchanged

## **3\. Universal Pre-Submission Checklist**

Regardless of which platform you're submitting to next, confirm these before opening the application:

* \[ \] Manuscript sample meets or exceeds that platform's stated minimum (5k words for GoodNovel/LetterLux; 10 chapters \+ 10k words for MoboReader; unconfirmed for SevenCat/Dreame: check first)  
* \[ \] First chapters specifically polished, not just "the whole draft is fine": every platform in this set uses your opening chapters as the actual signing decision, not the manuscript as a whole  
* \[ \] Genre checked against that platform's accepted list (hard list on LetterLux; classification system on MoboReader; unrestricted-but-verify on the others)  
* \[ \] Confirmed the work is not fan fiction of non-public-domain IP, if that platform bans it (GoodNovel, MoboReader explicitly; unconfirmed elsewhere)  
* \[ \] AI-assist level checked against that platform's tolerance, from GoodNovel's "grammar-fix only" leniency to SevenCat's zero-tolerance and MoboReader's submission-blocking gate  
* \[ \] Chapter length adjusted to that platform's format norm before uploading (don't paste MoboReader-length chapters into a LetterLux submission unchanged, or vice versa)  
* \[ \] Cover art ready and checked against content restrictions, where the platform states any (GoodNovel is explicit; others unconfirmed)  
* \[ \] A completion/ending outline ready to submit if that platform asks for one (confirmed requirement on GoodNovel)
