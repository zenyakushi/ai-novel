# Novel 1: Story-State Ledger

Per-chapter deltas, in order. The live Story-State Ledger is the last Master
Ledger Update plus every delta since. No Master Ledger Update has fired yet:
the first checkpoint is due at batch end or immediately after the next major
event, whichever lands first.

## Chapter 1 delta

* Wren Ashveil's mate bond (a thread of gold-white light) ignites publicly between her and Alpha Kieran Draven during the Rite, witnessed by the full amphitheater  
* Established: Wren has secretly carried an unexplained "second pulse" her whole life, hidden per her late mother's instruction to "stay unremarkable"  
* New: King Aldric's reaction to the ignition reads as knowing, not shocked, and Wren consciously notices this reads as wrong, an early unresolved thread  
* Oracle Yseult shows a visible flash of composure-break at the bond's ignition before resetting her expression, hinting she knows something isn't right  
* New named characters introduced: Garrick (Kieran's beta) and Lira (Kieran's sister), both present at the Rite  
* Cold open confirmed as a flash-forward: the branding/rejection has not yet happened in the main timeline; the story has now caught up to the moment right before it

## Chapter 2 delta

* Locked in: King Aldric privately told Oracle Yseult before the Rite that it "must go as planned" with "no deviations," confirming the rejection was orchestrated in advance rather than a spontaneous ritual outcome.  
* Locked in: Yseult publicly halted the Rite by citing a file the Elders pulled on Wren's bloodline that same morning, declaring it "flagged" and "unfit." This is the Sanctum's stated justification for the rejection.  
* New mechanic: Yseult deliberately let the bond fully ignite and be witnessed by the entire amphitheater before intervening, so the rejection cannot later be challenged as pre-planned rather than ritual-driven. This is the Sanctum's method for making political decisions look procedurally legitimate.  
* New physical evidence: the rejection scroll handed to Kieran had its wax seal already broken and re-sealed before he ever left his chambers that morning, meaning the command was written before the Rite began.  
* Relationship beat: Garrick and Lira both react with visible shock, not recognition, at the ignition, establishing neither is complicit in the Sanctum's plan at this point in the story.

## Chapter 3 delta

* Locked in: Kieran spoke the full formal rejection aloud, but visibly hesitated (stalled mouth, throat working, one hand sliding toward her before catching himself) before finishing "unfit," his first conscious crack of doubt while still complying.   
* Locked in: The bond's forced severance triggered a violent physical backlash. The thread of light snapped back into Wren, throwing her to the platform and knocking the breath from her.   
* New: Wren's dormant "second pulse" reacted for the first time on page during the backlash, beating harder as the brand formed. She still doesn't understand what it is.   
* New: A mark began forming at Wren's collarbone from beneath the skin moments after the recoil hit, confirming the main timeline has now caught up to the branding image from the Chapter 1 cold open.   
* Relationship beat: Lira tried to move toward Wren and was physically blocked by a guard, establishing her sympathy is real but she isn't yet free (or willing) to act on it.

## Chapter 4 delta

* Locked in: Oracle Yseult performed the sealing rite over the brand, declaring "The bond is broken. The mark is sealed." A sealed mark is treated as beyond challenge: nobody in the amphitheater argued, and the narration establishes nobody ever does.
* New mechanic: the brand's legal effect was stated aloud as law. No pack owes Wren shelter or hearth, and any pack that crosses her path is bound to report the sighting rather than offer quarter. The mark is a pre-existing, recognized warning in this world, not an invention for her case.
* Locked in: King Aldric publicly invoked the Exile Law and set the deadline himself. Wren crosses the Wilds' border before dawn, with no trial and no chance to speak. He ordered Garrick to walk her to the gate.
* New: Aldric's tension visibly eased once the sentence landed, the same knowing look Wren registered at the ignition in Chapter 1. She now consciously connects the two, hardening the orchestration thread from suspicion toward conviction.
* Relationship beat: Lira escalated from blocked to actively restrained, calling out for someone to help Wren and being seized by a guard mid-sentence. Garrick took Wren's arm carefully, told her "Don't give them a reason to drag you," and would not meet her eyes, his first crack in duty-first compliance. Kieran twice moved to speak and stopped himself both times.

## Chapter 5 delta

* New mechanic: the brand's social effect is immediate and total inside Duskmoor. Lamps went out and doors closed ahead of Wren as she was walked out, including Mira the baker, who had shown her kindness for three winters and turned her child's face away rather than be seen looking. The warning-mark works without anyone enforcing it.
* Locked in: Garrick escorted Wren to the outer gate and handed her to four Sanctum enforcers there, per Aldric's order. Before letting go he used her name, not her bloodline, and told her to keep moving once through the border and not to stop to rest. She asked whether that was Kieran's message or his own. He did not answer.
* New: Wren began cataloguing landmarks along the exile road (a split boulder where the track bends north, a lightning-scarred dead pine, the point where the river noise drops behind the ridge, and four more). This is her first act of intent since the rejection, and she frames it as a route back.
* New: Wren consciously registered the second pulse for the first time since the platform, noting it keeps its own time, slower and steadier than her own, and that she has spent nineteen years pretending not to feel it.
* Cliffhanger, locked in: past the last checkpoint the lead enforcer gathered the other three and was overheard saying "Before first light." Asked what happens if she is found, he answered "The Wilds took her. That is all that goes in the file." The kill order is deniable by design and is not in writing.

## Chapter 6 delta

* MAJOR, locked in: the four Sanctum enforcers attacked Wren inside the Wilds, downhill of the border, deliberately so the body would read as having run. She was struck at the back of the skull and left bleeding in the snow. The enforcers left without checking her, one of them saying she was not going anywhere.
* MAJOR new power beat: the lead enforcer used a full Alpha command ("Kneel") on Wren and it failed, twice. She had felt the same command shape twice before in her life and folded both times without deciding to. This is the first on-page evidence of her bloodline's resistance to Alpha commands, and it was witnessed by all four enforcers, who were visibly unsettled by it. None of them can explain it, and neither can she.
* New: Wren fought back and injured at least two enforcers, breaking one man's nose with a heel strike and drawing blood from another with her nails and her teeth. She has never struck anyone before. What disturbs her is not that it worked but that her body chose it without consulting her and knew where the target would be.
* New: Wren sustained a significant wound along her ribs, plus a head injury, and was bleeding into the snow when the enforcers left. She was crawling uphill toward the split boulder she had catalogued in Chapter 5 when she was caught.
* Thread opened: at least two enforcers carry visible injuries from this, which is physical evidence that contradicts any clean "the Wilds took her" report. Relevant to Chapter 7, where the file is marked closed on their word.
* Status at chapter end: Wren is alone, wounded, bleeding, and losing consciousness in the snow past the border. The second pulse is still beating, slower than her heart and steadier.

## MASTER LEDGER UPDATE (checkpoint fired at Chapter 6)

First checkpoint of the novel. Triggered by a major event rather than batch end:
Chapter 6 contains an attempt on Wren's life and the first on-page evidence of
her bloodline. This consolidates Chapters 1 through 6 and replaces the deltas
above as the live Story-State Ledger. Deltas resume from Chapter 7.

**Wren Ashveil**

* Publicly bonded to Alpha Kieran Draven at the Rite, then publicly rejected by him, then branded unfit and sealed by Oracle Yseult.
* Exiled under the Exile Law by King Aldric, who set the deadline himself: across the Wilds' border before dawn.
* Currently: alone past the border, wounded along the ribs, struck at the back of the skull, bleeding into snow and losing consciousness. Not yet rescued.
* Carries an unexplained "second pulse," hidden since childhood on her late mother's instruction to "stay unremarkable." It reacted during the bond's severance and has been beating steadily and out of step with her heart since.
* Proven resistant to a full Alpha command, twice, in front of four witnesses. Neither she nor they can explain it.
* Has begun cataloguing landmarks along the exile road, framed to herself as a route back. First act of intent since the rejection.
* Does not know her bloodline's true nature.

**What the Sanctum did, and what is provable**

* The rejection was orchestrated in advance. Aldric told Yseult before the Rite that it "must go as planned" with "no deviations."
* Yseult let the bond fully ignite and be witnessed before halting the Rite, so the outcome would read as ritual-driven rather than political.
* The stated justification was a bloodline file the Elders pulled that same morning.
* Physical evidence: the rejection scroll handed to Kieran had its wax seal already broken and re-sealed before he left his chambers, meaning the command was written before the Rite began.
* The kill order was verbal and deniable. "The Wilds took her. That is all that goes in the file."
* Counter-evidence now exists: at least two enforcers carry injuries from Wren, which a clean report cannot account for.

**Kieran Draven**

* Spoke the formal rejection, visibly hesitating before the word "unfit." First conscious crack of doubt while still complying.
* Did not intervene at the sealing or the sentencing. Moved to speak twice and stopped himself both times.
* Does not yet know his upbringing was engineered to produce this outcome.

**Others**

* Aldric: knew the outcome in advance. His tension eased visibly once the sentence landed, which Wren noticed and has now connected to his reaction at the ignition.
* Yseult: halted the Rite and sealed the brand. Showed one flash of composure-break at the ignition.
* Lira: sympathetic from the start. Escalated from blocked by a guard to physically restrained after calling out for someone to help Wren.
* Garrick: enforced the rejection out of duty, then used Wren's name at the gate and warned her to keep moving and not stop to rest. Would not say whether the warning came from Kieran. First crack in his compliance.
* The four enforcers: unnamed. Attacked Wren downhill of the border so the body would read as having run. Left without confirming the kill.

**Open threads**

* Who gave the verbal kill order, and how far above the enforcers it originated.
* Whether Garrick's warning was his own or Kieran's.
* The nature of the second pulse and the command resistance.
* The older mark beneath the brand, not yet seen by anyone (due Chapter 9).
* The enforcers' injuries versus whatever they report (due Chapter 7).

**Established world rules in play**

* A sealed mark is beyond challenge. No pack owes a branded wolf shelter or hearth, and any pack crossing her path must report the sighting rather than offer quarter.
* The warning-mark is recognized on sight and predates Wren's case.
* The Wilds are lawless country past the border, not a wall or a boundary feature.

## Chapter 7 delta

* New character on page: Archon Malrick Veyra, at the Sanctum Spire. He reads the morning returns standing at a window slit because the angle makes poor penmanship obvious. Thirty-one years in the post.
* Locked in: the enforcer captain filed Wren's death as exposure and closed the file the same hour he opened it. Veyra caught the arithmetic. The border road is nine miles, they left an hour past midnight, and the filing arrived at dawn, which does not allow time to walk her out, wait, and return.
* New: the Bloodline Registry entry reads "Ashveil, W. Duskmoor. Maternal line unverified. Flagged pending Rite." Veyra set that flag himself nineteen years ago. He has set four hundred and six such flags in his career and says three ever mattered.
* New mechanic: Veyra distinguishes a common bond, which flickers and dies, from one that ignites, holds, and has to be cut. He calls the second an event, and an event that produces no detail in its paperwork is what makes him doubt the report.
* New: he commended the enforcers in writing, to be read aloud to other companies, specifically so they would feel settled and not suspect they were being checked.
* Cliffhanger, locked in: he sent for his own tracker, Ilsa, by the east stair rather than the registry floor, sealed the order with an unwarmed ring so the mark is hard to read, and ordered the Duskmoor file left closed because closed files are not reviewed.
* New named characters: Sound (Veyra's clerk), Ilsa (Veyra's personal tracker, not yet on page).

* Cliffhanger, locked in: Tamsin states the trap plainly. Sent back across the border, Wren is finished inside a week. Left alone in the Wilds with the mark and the blood, she does not last a month, and it will not be the Sanctum that does it. There is no door Tamsin can put her out of.
